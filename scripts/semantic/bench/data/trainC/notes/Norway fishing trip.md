# Norway fishing trip

Lofoten, 12-19 September, with Dad and Marek. Rorbu (fisherman's cabin) in Henningsvær, 6 beds, 14 500 NOK for the week.

- Flights Kraków - Oslo - Leknes with SAS; checked bag 23 kg only, rods in tubes as special baggage (350 NOK each way)
- Fishing: sea fishing from the boat (rented 17 ft, 25 hp, 2800 NOK/day) - cod, pollock, haddock
- Tackle: jigging rod 20-30 lb, 2500 reel, 50 lb braid, pilkers 150-300 g, glow shrimp rigs, circle hooks
- Filleting at the cabin table; freezing max 15 kg per person for export (we need a cooler)
- Safety: life jackets mandatory, check tide and wind (Windguru), no shore trips in rain
- Budget per person: flights 1100 PLN, cabin share 560 PLN, boat share 350, food 700

- [ ] check rules: no licence needed for sea fishing from a boat, but don't sell fish
- [ ] book car rental (Toyota Aygo 800 NOK/day)
