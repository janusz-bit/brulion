# Phrasal verbs - my list

Learning from real emails at work, not from lists. Here are the ones I keep getting wrong:

- follow up on (= wrócić do tematu): "I'll follow up on this tomorrow"
- come across (= natknąć się): "I came across an old photo"
- put off (= odkładać): "stop putting off the call"
- get along with, run out of, set up, turn down (= odrzucić ofertę)
- look forward to + -ing ("I look forward to hearing from you") - not "to hear"!

Tips from Sarah (teacher on italki, Tuesdays 8 pm, 14 USD/30 min): don't translate, learn each one inside a full sentence and say it aloud three times.

Mistake pattern: I say "discuss about" -> wrong, just "discuss the plan".
