# Street photography in Lisbon

Four days in March (11-14). Notes for myself:

- Alfama early in the morning, 7:00-9:00, tram 28 is crowded after 9
- Shoot 35 mm, f/8, zone focus at 3 m, auto ISO up to 1600 - no time to chase focus
- LX Factory on Saturday, murals, a lot of tourists, good for silhouettes
- Miradouro da Senhora do Monte at sunset, queue by 18:30
- Always ask before photographing street vendors, most say yes with a smile; a woman selling ginjinha said no, fine
- Memory: 3 cards, 128 GB, shot 1400 frames, kept 60
- Pastéis de Belém at 8:30, line short; 1.30 EUR each

Lesson: wear light shoes, cobblestones everywhere. Backpack must not look like a camera bag.
