# Milky Way shoot

Plan for Bieszczady, new moon window 21-24 August. Bortle 2-3 up there, hopefully no clouds.

- Spot: Połonina Wetlińska, parking at Przełęcz Wyżna, 40 min walk with headlamp (red mode!)
- Gear: A6400 + Samyang 12 mm f/2, tripod, intervalometer, spare batteries (cold drains them)
- Settings: 20 s, f/2, ISO 3200, manual focus on a star using live view zoom. The 500 rule gives ~28 s but stars trail on 24 Mpx
- Check Clear Outside + Windy two days before, Milky Way core rises at 23:10 in the south
- Stack 10 frames in Sequator for noise, then Lightroom
