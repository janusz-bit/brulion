# Velo Dunajec trip

Plan for 3 days in June (13-15), with Michał. About 200 km total, mostly flat and asphalt, a few gravel bits.

- Day 1: Nowy Targ -> Szczawnica, 70 km, ends with the climb to the Pieniny gate; sleep at pension "U Zośki"
- Day 2: Szczawnica -> Czorsztyn (ferry!) -> Niedzica castle, 55 km, lunch pierogi
- Day 3: Czorsztyn -> Nowy Sącz by the river, 75 km. Train back from Nowy Sącz 18:12 (bikes allowed, reserve bike ticket 10 PLN)

Notes:

- Panniers or frame bag? Frame bag + saddle bag, max 6 kg
- Spare tube x2, multi-tool, quick link
- Rain forecast on day 2 - bring a thin jacket
- [ ] book pension (2 rooms, 180 PLN each)
- [ ] print tickets for the ferry
