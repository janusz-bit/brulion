Weeknight recipe, serves 3, 30 minutes.

- 2 chicken breasts, sliced
- 2 tbsp green curry paste (Mae Ploy is the best I found)
- 400 ml coconut milk
- 1 aubergine, green beans, a handful of Thai basil
- fish sauce, lime juice, a little brown sugar

Fry the paste in a spoon of the thick coconut cream until fragrant, add chicken, then the rest of the milk and vegetables. Simmer 12 min. Serve with jasmine rice. Too spicy last time — use 1.5 tbsp.
