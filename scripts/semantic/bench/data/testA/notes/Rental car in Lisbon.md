## Lisbon trip — car rental

- picked up a Fiat 500 at the airport, Sixt desk, terminal 1
- deposit blocked on the card: €900, released after 7 days
- toll roads: need the **Via Verde** transponder (+€1.50/day plus tolls), otherwise fines
- fuel: return with a full tank, the nearest petrol station is in Moscavide
- parking in Alfama is a nightmare, we left the car at the Parque da Figueira, €18 per day
- full insurance with zero excess was €12/day extra — worth it, the streets are tiny
