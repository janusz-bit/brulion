# Garden plan 2026

Plan for the plot behind the house (~300 m²).

- **Raised beds**: build three 1.2 × 3 m beds from larch boards, fill with a mix of topsoil and compost
- Beds: carrots + onions, beetroot + lettuce, beans + courgettes
- Order the seeds in January: Torseed and Legutko
- Water barrel under the gutter — 300 l
- Gravel path between the beds
- bird feeder and a small hedgehog house in the corner
- [ ] mark the beds with string in March
- [ ] ask the neighbour about the shared fence
