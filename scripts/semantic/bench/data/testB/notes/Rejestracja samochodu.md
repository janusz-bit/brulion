# Registering the car

Bought a used Skoda Octavia on 5 June (from a private seller, 28 500 PLN). Deadline for registration: 30 days.

- documents: sales contract, old registration certificate, technical inspection stamp, OC insurance
- fee: 256.50 PLN (registration) + plates 80 PLN
- visit the Wydział Komunikacji, ticket machine at the entrance
- tax on civil law transactions (PCC-3): 2%, i.e. 570 PLN, within 14 days
- [x] buy insurance before registering
- [ ] pay PCC-3 online via the tax office website
- [ ] book the vehicle inspection in case it has expired
