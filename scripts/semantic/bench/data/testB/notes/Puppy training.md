# Puppy training

Our new puppy, Nala (golden retriever, 12 weeks). Notes from the training school on Sundays at 10:00 in the park.

- sit, down and come: reward with a treat within 2 seconds
- leash walking: stop when she pulls, walk on when the leash is loose
- crate at night — whining for the first week, then fine
- no jumping on guests: turn away and ignore
- socialization: meet 3 new dogs per week, ride the tram once
- accidents on the carpet 2 times this week, take her out every 2 hours
- trainer Ewa (tel. 798 100 456) recommends clicker
