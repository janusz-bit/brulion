# Project Hail Mary

Andy Weir. Audiobook, 16 hours, listened while running and in the car.

- an astronaut wakes up on a ship with no memory — the structure of slowly learning who he is works really well
- Rocky, the alien engineer who talks in musical chords: best character of the year
- science explained so you feel clever, but not too heavy
- the ending made me tear up on the bus (embarrassing)
- Movie with Ryan Gosling is coming, hopefully they keep the humour
Score: 10/10, buying the paper copy for Michał.
