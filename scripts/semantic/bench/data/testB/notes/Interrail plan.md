# Interrail plan

Idea for next summer: 3 weeks with a Global Pass (flexible, 10 days within 2 months), about 330 EUR.

Draft route:
1. Wrocław → Prague (2 nights)
2. Prague → Vienna → Budapest
3. Night train to Zagreb (couchette booked ahead, seat reservation costs extra)
4. Ljubljana, then Venice
5. back via Munich

- reservations are compulsory on night trains and some high speed lines
- download the Rail Planner app, works offline
- travel light: one 35 L backpack
- ask Jarek if he wants to join for the first week
