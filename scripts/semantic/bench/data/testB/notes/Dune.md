# Dune — Frank Herbert

Finished last night, 3rd time reading it. Still amazing.

- politics, religion and ecology mixed together, nothing is only background
- Paul's path from a boy to a messiah is actually a warning, not a triumph
- favourite quote: "Fear is the mind-killer."
- the movie by Villeneuve (part 1 and 2) is visually great, but the book has more inner monologue
- next up: Dune Messiah, I heard it is darker
Rating 9/10. Lend to Bartek? He asked twice.
