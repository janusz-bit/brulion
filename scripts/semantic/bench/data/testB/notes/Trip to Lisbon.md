# Trip to Lisbon

Long weekend, 3–6 October. Flight from Warsaw with TAP, 289 PLN return (cheap!).

- stay: small hostel in Alfama, close to the tram 28 stop
- must see: Belém tower, pastel de nata at the famous bakery (queue ~30 min), LX Factory
- day trip to Sintra by train from Rossio, 40 min
- fado night in a small bar, booked for Saturday 21:00
- buy the Viva Viagem card, the metro is cheap
- weather forecast: 22°C, sunny
Note: the hills are brutal, take comfortable shoes!
