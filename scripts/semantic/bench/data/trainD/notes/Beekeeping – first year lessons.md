# Beekeeping – first year lessons

Looking back on my first season with 2 colonies (Carniolan, bought as nucs from Mr. Wójcik).

What worked:
- Keeping a log: date, weather, frames of brood, queen seen Y/N
- Smoker with dry pine needles and a bit of cardboard – burns cool and long
- Inspect between 11 and 15 on warm days, never when it's windy or about to rain
- Sugar syrup 1:1 in spring, 2:1 in autumn

Mistakes:
- Opened the hive too often (every 5 days) — the colonies got irritated
- Forgot to put a queen excluder under the honey super → brood in the honey frames
- Late varroa treatment (mid-October) → lost one colony in January
- Wore sandals once. Never again!

Costs: starting kit 1 850 PLN (suit, gloves, hive tool, smoker), 2 hives 1 240 PLN, nucs 760 PLN.
First honey: 22 kg, tasted like linden and clover.
Next year: join the local club (Koło Pszczelarzy Zawoja, monthly meeting at the fire station), build a mating nuc.
