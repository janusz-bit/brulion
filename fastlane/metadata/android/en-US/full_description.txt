Brulion keeps your notes as plain Markdown files in a folder you choose. Write them as a block outliner with daily journals, link pages and blocks, plan with a calendar, tasks, reminders and alarms, and calculate right inside your notes.

• Works offline; your files stay readable by Obsidian and Logseq
• Daily journals, backlinks, tags, search and queries
• Calendar, tasks, recurring reminders and alarms that ring even when the app is closed
• Calculations with units, percentages and table formulas
• Drawings in notes, with pressure-sensitive stylus support
• Sync through Syncthing (a plain folder) or Nextcloud (WebDAV)
• Notes from a GitHub repository as a notebook: new versions downloaded, your changes committed or proposed as pull requests
• Home screen widgets and "Share → Brulion"
• Interface in English and Polish

A Syncthing folder on the shared storage needs the "All files access" permission; a notebook in the app's private folder synced with Nextcloud needs none.
