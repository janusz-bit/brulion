# Brulion Documentation

This documentation is made of ordinary Brulion notes — a separate notebook called “Brulion Documentation”. You can read it, click its links and even edit it: it is your own copy. The examples in it are live, so the best way to learn is to try changing them. To get back to your own notes, use the “Switch notebook” button (the first letter of the notebook's name at the top of the icon bar on the left) or press Ctrl+Shift+B.

Brulion updates the documentation together with the app: new pages are added, and pages you haven't changed get the new version. Pages you have changed or deleted stay the way you left them. This notebook doesn't fire reminders (your own notebook's alarms keep working), quick captures don't go into it, and the app doesn't reopen it by itself when it starts again.
